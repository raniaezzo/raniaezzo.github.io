My research aims to uncover how the human visual system encodes information about space and time. A few topics
I currently focus on include:
- Asymmmetries of motion and orientation processing
- Infleunce of global context on local neural activity
- Topographic & functional organization of the visual cortex
- Temporal profile of oculomotor responses

My past and present research experience spans both academic and clinical settings, and involves a combination of psychophysics, neuroimaging, eye tracking, and computational modeling. I have taught undergraduates, several topics related to visual neuroscience and programming. Aside from my empirical research aims and teaching, I have a keen interest in philosophy and history of science, and I am additionally committed to advancing reproducible science.
